About the Artist

Gail Wager lives in the mountains of Colorado and has been painting for more than fifty years. Her watercolors follow the life around her — elk and foxes in the high country, columbines in summer, the pueblos of the Southwest, and the people and animals she knows and loves.

She earned her master's degree in art education, and for the last thirty years she has been studying and teaching painting in Colorado, sharing her craft with generations of students. Her recent work explores acrylic and mixed media collage.
