Site Analytics

About this report

Visits, pageviews and artwork interest (views and stars) are recorded anonymously. Numbers shown here come from this browser's own history of the site. To collect site-wide totals with visitor regions, connect a free GoatCounter account by setting GOATCOUNTER_CODE in js/main.js — its dashboard then provides worldwide visit counts by country, and this weekly report can be refreshed from its export. See the repository README for details.
